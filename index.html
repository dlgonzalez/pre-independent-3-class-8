Phase 5: Final Project

Oral Presentation Planner 📊

It's time to prepare your final presentation for this unit. Choose a topic, do some quick research, and use the blueprint below to organize your speech.

Topic Ideas

  • • Should humans live on Mars someday?
  • • The most important space missions in history
  • • Life inside the International Space Station
  • • The dangers astronauts face in space
  • • What would happen if humans disappeared?
  • • Could aliens exist somewhere in the universe?
  • • The future of space tourism
  • • Black holes: space’s biggest mystery

1. Introduction Hook the audience

Phrases: "Did you know that...", "Today I'd like to talk about..."

2. Body Paragraphs 2-3 Main Points

Phrases: "Firstly...", "Moving on to my next point...", "Another interesting fact is..."

3. Conclusion Summary & Q&A

Phrases: "To sum up...", "Thank you for listening. Are there any questions?"
